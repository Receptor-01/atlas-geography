HOW TO USE

Learn countries, flags, and capitals one question at a time.

FLAGLook at the flag and choose the country it belongs to.
SHAPELook at the country outline and choose the correct country.
CAPITALLook at the capital city and choose its country.

Answer a questionClick one of the answer buttons. The correct country, capital, flag, and location will then be shown.

Active and StandbyUse Active mode to answer yourself. Standby mode automatically plays questions for passive learning.

Change difficultyMove your pointer to the side of the window to reveal the difficulty slider. Choose between 2 and 8 answers.

Move between questionsUse the left and right arrow buttons beside the preview to revisit or skip a question.
